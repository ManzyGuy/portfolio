Methodology

How I Work

A disciplined 6-stage lifecycle approach ensuring technology directly serves operational goals and real people.

01

Understand

I begin by understanding the organisation, its users, and the root problem. This includes identifying existing processes, requirements, pain points, and the desired operational outcome.

02

Analyse

I examine the current workflow and determine where technology can add value. This involves analysing information flows, identifying inefficiencies, and assessing existing systems.

03

Design

I translate requirements into a practical solution — whether that involves designing a normalized database, robust application architecture, user interface, or workflow schema.

04

Implement

I develop and configure the solution using appropriate technologies. The objective is never complexity for its own sake, but building something resilient and tailored to requirements.

05

Enable

Technology only succeeds when people can use it effortlessly. I provide technical documentation, user guidance, and hands-on ICT training to foster enthusiastic adoption.

06

Improve

A solution is never finished simply because it is deployed. I proactively look for opportunities to enhance functionality, reliability, and alignment with evolving organisation needs.
